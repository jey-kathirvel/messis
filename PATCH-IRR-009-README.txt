PATCH-IRR-009 — Irrigation Alerts & Notifications

Adds an owner-scoped operational alert center, automated scans for overdue and
upcoming schedules, low water levels, pump/equipment attention, fertigation
approval, fertilizer stock and existing weather/execution risks. Includes
acknowledge, resolve, dismiss and reopen workflows plus a printable report.

Deployment: run ./apply_patch.sh from /opt/messis.
